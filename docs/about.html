About Us

Our story

Gospel Lighthouse Christian Fellowship had its beginnings in October 1962 as Toronto Gospel Lighthouse in Toronto, Ontario and has always been a multicultural church with members and visitors from around the world.

The men of God who have shepherded at the Toronto Gospel Lighthouse over the years have helped to train up several pastors, evangelists and missionaries. Also, the Lighthouse takes great joy in consistently supporting missionary endeavors around the world.

In 2017, the Lighthouse transitioned from Toronto to Ajax, Ontario and consequently changed the name to Gospel Lighthouse Christian Fellowship.

With this move, the passion for missions remains. Fueling this passion is the continual expositional preaching and teaching through God’s Word.

All are welcome to join us for Christian worship, powerful preaching, and a wonderful fellowship of love!

What we believe

We, the members of Gospel Lighthouse Christian Fellowship, affirm our shared commitment to the foundational truths of the Christian faith as revealed in Holy Scripture and embodied in the life and teachings of Jesus Christ.

Our statement of faith serves as a guide for our beliefs, worship, and practice, and reflects our commitment to living out the gospel in our community and beyond.

Read our statement of faith

Our core values

Gospel Lighthouse Christian Fellowship is a vibrant, welcoming community dedicated to sharing the love of Christ. We strive to be a beacon of light, spreading faith, hope, and love to all who walk through our doors.

We believe in the power of the Gospel to transform lives. Our core beliefs center around faith in Jesus Christ, the authority of Scripture, and the importance of loving God and others. We are committed to making disciples and sharing God’s Word with the world.

You’ll find a warm and friendly atmosphere, uplifting worship, and a message that speaks to your everyday life. Whether you’re new to church or have been attending for years, you are welcome here just as you are.

There are many ways to get involved at Gospel Lighthouse Christian Fellowship! You can join a ministry group, volunteer, attend events, or participate in small groups. Whether you’re looking to serve or grow in your faith, there’s an opportunity for you to make a difference.

You can connect with us by email at info@glcfellowship.ca.
